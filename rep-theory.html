Topics in Representation Theory

University of Southern Denmark, Fall 2026.

Meeting time: Wednesdays and Fridays, 10 am – 12 pm.

Location: QM Lounge.

References

  • J.-P. Serre, Complex Semisimple Lie Algebras, Springer Monographs in Mathematics, Springer, 2001.
  • P. Etingof, Lie Groups and Lie Algebras, I, lecture notes for MIT 18.745.
  • E. Frenkel and D. Ben-Zvi, Vertex Algebras and Algebraic Curves, 2nd ed., Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 88, AMS, 2004.

Topics of Presentation

  • From Lie algebra representations to Lie group representations.
  • The Peter–Weyl theorem.
  • Representations of algebraic groups (SLn, GLn).
  • The Borel–Weil theorem for SL2.
  • Category 𝒪 for 𝔰𝔩2.
  • Representations of the symmetric group Sn and Schur–Weyl duality.
  • Quantum groups: representations of Uq(𝔰𝔩2).
